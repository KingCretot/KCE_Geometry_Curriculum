Let's Begin

How much wrapping paper does it take?

Surface area is the total area of every surface on a solid, the amount of paper it would take to wrap it with no overlap. Today we unfold prisms and cylinders flat, find the formulas hiding in the unfolded shapes, and use them in both directions.

By the end of today, you will be able to:

  • 1Explain where the surface area formulas for prisms and cylinders come from
  • 2Calculate the lateral area and surface area of a prism or cylinder
  • 3Find a missing dimension when the surface area is known

Vocabulary

Bases, lateral faces, and nets

A RIGHT RECTANGULAR PRISM

The two congruent, parallel faces are the bases. The other faces are lateral faces, and their total area is the lateral area (L). Add the bases and you get the surface area (S). In a right prism, the lateral faces are rectangles and the height (h) is the length of a side edge.

A net is the flat pattern you get by unfolding a solid. The surface area formulas for prisms, cylinders, pyramids, and cones all come from looking at a net.

Core Formula

Surface area of a right prism

Right Prism
L = Ph     S = Ph + 2B

Why L = Ph: unfold the lateral faces and they line up into one long rectangle. Its width is the distance around the base, the perimeter P, and its height is h. Then add the two bases, each with area B.

Core Formula

Surface area of a right cylinder

Right Cylinder
L = 2πrh     S = 2πrh + 2πr²
RADIUS r, HEIGHT h

Peel the label off a soup can and it unrolls into a rectangle. Its width is the circumference of the can, 2πr, and its height is h.

It’s the prism formula in disguise: the “perimeter” of a circle base is its circumference 2πr, and each base has area πr².

Worked Example

A prism and a cylinder

Problem: (a) A rectangular prism has a 5 × 3 base and height 4. (b) A cylinder has radius 3 and height 10. Find each surface area.

1
Prism: perimeter and base
P = 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16, and B = 5 × 3 = 15
2
Prism: surface area
S = Ph + 2B = 16(4) + 2(15) = 64 + 30 = 94 square units
3
Cylinder: lateral area
L = 2π(3)(10) = 60π
4
Cylinder: surface area
S = 60π + 2π(3)² = 60π + 18π = 78π ≈ 245.0 square units

Worked Example

Working backward to a missing dimension

Problem: A cube has a surface area of 150 square inches. How long is each edge?

1
Use the structure of a cube
A cube has 6 congruent square faces, so each face has area 150 ÷ 6 = 25 square inches.
2
Find the edge
Each face is a square with area 25, so the edge is √25 = 5 inches.

The same idea works for a cylinder: if you know S and r, substitute both into S = 2πrh + 2πr² and solve for h.

Your Turn — Guided Practice

Now you try, with me right here

Use the surface area formulas.

Question 1 — A right prism has a right-triangle base with legs 3 and 4 and hypotenuse 5. Its height is 10. What is its surface area?

Here's why: P = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12, so L = 12 × 10 = 120. Each triangle base has B = ½(3)(4) = 6, and there are two: S = 120 + 12 = 132. 120 leaves out both bases, and 126 counts only one.

Question 2 — A cylinder has radius 2 and height 5. What is its surface area?

Here's why: L = 2π(2)(5) = 20π, and the two bases add 2π(2)² = 8π, for 28π total. 20π is only the lateral area.

Why This Matters

Every label, box, and can of paint

A cereal company pays for every square inch of cardboard in a box, so packaging designers compute surface area constantly. A painter estimating a room is finding the lateral area of a rectangular prism: the four walls, without the floor or ceiling.

Real world: the paper label on a soup can is exactly the lateral area of a cylinder, 2πrh, the rectangle you get when you unroll it.

Mini Knowledge Test

Show what you know — 5 quick questions

Question 1
The lateral area of a right prism is found with...
Here's why: Unfolded, the lateral faces form a rectangle P wide and h tall, so L = Ph. Bh is the volume, which you will meet in Lesson 9.4.
Question 2
A cube has edges of length 4. What is its surface area?
Here's why: Six square faces, each 4 × 4 = 16: 6 × 16 = 96. 64 is the volume, and 16 is just one face.
Question 3
A cylinder has radius 5 and height 4. What is its lateral area?
Here's why: L = 2πrh = 2π(5)(4) = 40π. 20π forgets the 2, and 50π is the area of the two bases.
Question 4
A cube has a surface area of 54 square units. How long is each edge?
Here's why: 54 ÷ 6 faces = 9 per face, and a square with area 9 has side 3. 9 is the area of one face, not the edge.
Question 5 — True or False
The lateral area of a cylinder includes the areas of its two circular bases.
Here's why: False. Lateral area covers only the curved side. Surface area is the lateral area plus the two bases.
