Let's Begin

Finding every side and angle

To solve a right triangle means to find all of its sides and angles. With trig ratios, you need only two pieces of information, as long as one is a side: one side and one acute angle, or two sides.

By the end of today, you will be able to:

  • 1Find a missing side using sine, cosine, or tangent
  • 2Find a missing angle using inverse trigonometric functions
  • 3Solve problems with angles of elevation and depression

Finding a Side

Pick the ratio that uses what you have

Problem: A right triangle has a hypotenuse of 20 and an acute angle of 35°. Find the side opposite the 35° angle and the side adjacent to it.

1
Opposite side
sin 35° = opp/20, so opp = 20 sin 35° ≈ 20(0.5736) ≈ 11.47
2
Adjacent side
cos 35° = adj/20, so adj = 20 cos 35° ≈ 20(0.8192) ≈ 16.38
3
Third angle
90° − 35° = 55°

Finding an Angle

Inverse trig functions

If you know a ratio and want the angle, use an inverse function: sin−1, cos−1, or tan−1. It answers the question “which angle has this ratio?”

Problem: The legs of a right triangle are 7 (opposite ∠A) and 10 (adjacent to ∠A). Find ∠A.

1
Choose the ratio
tan A = 7/10 = 0.7
2
Undo it
A = tan−1(0.7) ≈ 35.0°

Elevation and Depression

Angles measured from the horizontal

HOW TALL IS THE TREE?

The angle of elevation from your eye to the treetop is 40°, and you stand 50 ft from the tree.

1
Choose the ratio
The height is opposite the 40° angle, and 50 ft is adjacent: tan 40° = h/50
2
Solve
h = 50 tan 40° ≈ 50(0.8391) ≈ 42.0 ft (plus the height of your eyes above the ground)

Elevation vs. Depression

Two views of the same angle

The angle of elevation is measured up from the horizontal to an object above you. The angle of depression is measured down from the horizontal to an object below you.

The angle of depression from a lighthouse to a boat equals the angle of elevation from the boat to the lighthouse. The two horizontal lines are parallel, so these are alternate interior angles.

Watch Out

Common mistakes

1
Measuring from the vertical
Elevation and depression are always measured from the horizontal line, never from a wall or a pole.
2
Wrong calculator mode
In radian mode, sin 30 gives about −0.988 instead of 0.5. Check for DEG on the screen.
3
Using sin−1 to find a side
Inverse functions give angles. To find a side, use sin, cos, or tan.

Your Turn — Guided Practice

Now you try, with me right here

Solve the right triangle.

Question 1 — The leg adjacent to a 60° angle is 5. How long is the hypotenuse?

Here's why: cos 60° = 5/h, so h = 5 ÷ 0.5 = 10. 2.5 multiplies by cos 60° instead of dividing.

Question 2 — Both legs of a right triangle are 1. What is the measure of each acute angle?

Here's why: tan A = 1/1 = 1, and tan−1(1) = 45°. Equal legs always make a 45-45-90 triangle.

Why This Matters

Measuring without climbing

Foresters measure the height of trees with a clinometer, a tool that reads the angle of elevation. Pilots use the angle of descent to land: a typical approach drops 3° below horizontal, about 318 feet of height lost for every nautical mile.

Real world: surveyors use a theodolite to measure angles to landmarks, then use trig to map land, plan roads, and set property lines.

Mini Knowledge Test

Show what you know — 5 quick questions

Question 1
A right triangle has a hypotenuse of 10 and a 30° angle. How long is the side opposite the 30° angle?
Here's why: opp = 10 sin 30° = 10(0.5) = 5. 8.66 is the adjacent side, 10 cos 30°.
Question 2
A 12-ft ladder leans against a wall, making a 70° angle with the ground. About how high up the wall does it reach?
Here's why: The height is opposite the 70° angle: 12 sin 70° ≈ 12(0.9397) ≈ 11.3 ft. 4.1 ft is the distance from the wall, 12 cos 70°.
Question 3
If sin A = 0.5 and A is acute, what is A?
Here's why: sin−1(0.5) = 30°. cos 60° also equals 0.5, but the question gives the sine.
Question 4
Why does the angle of depression from a lighthouse to a boat equal the angle of elevation from the boat?
Here's why: Both angles are measured from horizontal lines, which are parallel, and the line of sight is a transversal.
Question 5 — True or False
To find an angle when you know two sides of a right triangle, you use an inverse trig function.
Here's why: True. Form the ratio of the two sides, then use sin−1, cos−1, or tan−1.
